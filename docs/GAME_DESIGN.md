# LABIJ

First-person retro PS1-inspired cooperative dungeon roguelike for 1–8 players. Target full expeditions: 2–4 hours with saves. Descend through floors and optional branches, gain XP and loot, collect runes, retrieve the Jewel and escape through a persistent dungeon.

Real-time tactical combat uses wind-up, active and recovery phases; spells compose reusable effects. Species and backgrounds affect starting capabilities and skill aptitudes; free skill training replaces fixed classes. Death allows costly revival. Between runs unlock cosmetics and alternative content, not permanent stat bonuses.

Difficulty depends on depth and branch, with encounter budgets adjusted for party size. The Jewel triggers escalating pursuit on the ascent. Handcrafted room templates complement procedural generation.

Prototype scope: movement and connectivity first; then persistent floors, combat, progression and a short complete expedition. Numerical balance remains provisional.

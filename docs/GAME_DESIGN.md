# LABIJ

First-person retro PS1-inspired cooperative dungeon roguelike for 1–8 players. Target full expeditions: 2–4 hours with saves. Descend through floors and rune branches, gain XP and loot, collect runes, retrieve the Jewel and escape through a persistent dungeon.

Real-time tactical combat uses wind-up, active and recovery phases; spells compose reusable effects. Species and backgrounds affect starting capabilities and skill aptitudes; domain XP and permanent talent purchases replace fixed classes. Any player death immediately loses the expedition for the whole party. No resurrection. Between runs unlock cosmetics and alternative content, not permanent stat bonuses.

Difficulty depends on depth and branch, with encounter budgets adjusted for party size. The Jewel triggers escalating pursuit on the ascent. Handcrafted room templates complement procedural generation.

Prototype scope: movement and connectivity first; then persistent floors, combat, progression and a short complete expedition. Numerical balance remains provisional.

First complete expedition: two shared boss runes unlock the sanctuary, the sentinel guards the Jewel, and extracting the single Jewel at the surface wins for the party, provided no player has died. The escape phase uses a finite pre-generated reserve and escalating pursuit rather than respawning enemies. No normal-run respec.

# Audio provenance

All bundled files below are CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is legally required; credit is retained for traceability. File names were changed for the stone and Kenney footsteps; audio bytes are unchanged from the cited mirrors. The game does not contact these mirrors at runtime.

- Fantozzi, *Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)*: six stone left/right samples. Original https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone ; upstream Freesound pack https://freesound.org/people/Fantozzi/packs/10338/ ; OGG redistribution https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit/tree/main/sfx/footsteps/Fantozzi-footsteps/ogg .
- Kenney, *RPG Audio*: four selected footstep samples. Original https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio ; redistribution https://github.com/euuuuuuan/moonshard-warden-public/tree/main/src/assets/kenney-rpg-audio .
- Kenney, *Impact Sounds*: two selected samples. Original https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds ; redistribution https://github.com/euuuuuuan/moonshard-warden-public/tree/main/src/assets/kenney-impact .
